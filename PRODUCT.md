# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Python package `trendlab` (src layout). The report website is a static site generated by the package itself (`trendlab site`) from the experiment outputs in `reports/` into `site/`, deployed to GitHub Pages by a GitHub Actions workflow. No JS framework; interactive charts are hand-written SVG rendered from exported JSON data.

## Users

Primary: quantitative-finance recruiters, hiring managers and interviewers at trading firms and systematic funds, skimming a student's portfolio project (Ashvik Mishra). They should understand the honest headline finding and the rigor behind it within about a minute, then be able to open any individual study and check the evidence.

## Product Purpose

`EMA-Crossover-Backtesting-Engine` started as a notebook claiming an EMA50/200 crossover on NVDA returned +505% vs +347% buy-and-hold. Version 2 is a bias-aware backtesting engine plus a ten-study research report showing that the outperformance was an artifact (warm-up and execution biases), that trend rules reliably cut drawdowns but do not beat buy-and-hold on risk-adjusted terms in any statistically significant way, and that trend following earns its keep only as a diversified multi-asset sleeve. Success: a reader comes away believing the author understands backtest hygiene, statistical inference and market mechanics.

## Positioning

The report argues against its own original result, reproducing the old claim exactly and then removing each bias step by step. Every number is generated by code from a committed data snapshot and is reproducible offline.

## Operating Context

Readers arrive from a CV, LinkedIn or GitHub link, usually on a laptop, sometimes on a phone. They skim, then spot-check one or two studies. The underlying artifacts are `reports/results.json`, `reports/tables/*.csv|md`, `reports/figures/*.png`, `docs/METHODOLOGY.md`, and the experiment modules `src/trendlab/experiments/e01…e10`.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Ten studies (e01 bias waterfall … e10 costs), each producing narrative text, tables, figures and a flat results dict.
- All copy and numbers are generated from computed results; the site must not hard-code numbers that the pipeline produces.
- The site must stay in sync with a re-run of `trendlab run`.
- Matplotlib figures use the `trendlab.plotting` palette (strategy = blue, buy-and-hold = neutral grey) on a light ground.

## Evidence on Hand

Real computed results only: `reports/results.json`, tables, 39 figures. No testimonials, users, employers or performance claims beyond these results may be invented.

## Product Principles

1. Honesty over salesmanship: negative and null results are reported as prominently as positive ones.
2. Proof next to every claim: a headline number always links to the chart or table that produced it.
3. Reproducible by construction: the site is a build output, not a hand-edited page.
4. Respect the reader's minute: verdict first, evidence on demand.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA contrast; charts carry text alternatives and data tables; keyboard-navigable; respects reduced motion.
